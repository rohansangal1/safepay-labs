# 005. Apache-2.0 license

- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-09-27

## Decision
License the code under Apache-2.0: permissive, with an explicit patent grant. That suits companies adopting the tool and leaves room for commercial offerings around it.

## Consequences
- Datasets and the paper may later carry separate terms (e.g. CC BY 4.0 for data). Decide at release.
- Generated subjects in `lab/subjects/agents/` are outputs of third-party AI tools. Check each tool's terms before redistributing them.
