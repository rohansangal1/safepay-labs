## Brand marks
The CC0 licence covers the icon files, not the trademarks. Stripe and Square are trademarks of
their owners. The marks are shown unaltered, only to name the provider formats this research
plans to emulate locally, next to a statement that the project is not affiliated with or endorsed
by either company. Do not recolour them into brand colours, combine them with our logo, or use
them in a way that suggests partnership. If either company asks, remove them.

## Photos
The Unsplash License allows free use without permission; attribution is appreciated, so the
photographers are credited in the page footer. Keep the credit line in `public/index.html` in step
with this table. The photos are resized and re-encoded (WebP) from the originals; nothing else is
altered.
