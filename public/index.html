Our mission

Apps should trust a payment message exactly as much as it deserves. We find out which designs make that safe, measure it honestly, and give the answers away.

  • Measure, don't assume

    We attack real code and record what the money did.

  • Count harm in money

    Outcomes a business would recognise.

  • Give it away

    Plan, paper, data and a free tool.

  • Fix first

    Maintainers hear privately before anyone else.

The problem

Four ways a payment message lies.

01

Forged

A message the payment company never sent.

02

Replayed

A real message, delivered again.

03

Reordered

A refund lands before the payment.

04

Mismatched

Right message, wrong order, amount or account.

What we measure

When a check is missing, it costs money.

  • Paid too little
  • Delivered twice
  • Order lost
  • Wrong account
  • Refund mismatch

Our approach

Build it. Attack it. Measure the money.

  1. 01

    Build a careful reference

    A checkout that handles every case, plus copies with one deliberate flaw each.

  2. 02

    Attack it locally

    Forged, replayed, reordered and mismatched messages, on our own machines.

  3. 03

    Measure the harm

    Money and orders, plus a score that never counts an untested check as a pass.
