Research boundaries

  • Owned fixtures first; provider sandboxes for compatibility.
  • Written authorization for any partner testing.
  • Public-page observations cannot certify backend security.
  • No live charges, real card data or unsolicited active probing.
  • PIAS is not PCI certification.
